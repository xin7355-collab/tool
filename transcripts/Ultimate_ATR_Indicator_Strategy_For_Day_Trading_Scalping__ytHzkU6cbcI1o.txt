AtrBands Indicator is another volatility-based tool, which creates bands around the price action using the average true range.

This indicator contains two bands that sit a certain distance away from the price action. This distance is determined by the ATR and a multiplier.

Think of it like a river with two banks. The price action is the water, and the ATR bands are the riverbanks, containing and guiding the flow.

The width of the ATR bands is dynamic, it changes based on the price volatility.When the price is more volatile, the ATR will be higher and the bands will be wider. When the price is less volatile, the ATR will be lower and the bands will be narrower.

So based on how much the price has been moving, the indicator offers you a reasonable range for where the price might go next.

It's all about watching the price action in relation to the bands. When the price is trading close to the upper band, it suggests that the market is in a strong uptrend.

If it touches the upper band, this could be a signal to consider taking profits or to be cautious about new long positions.

On the flip side, when the price is trading close to the lower band, it suggests that the market is in a strong downtrend.
